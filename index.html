How it works

Audit → Prioritize → Implement → Train

A simple sequence that puts the clearest, safest opportunities first.

1 · Audit

Review how work gets done today and where repeated tasks, data handling, and quality checks happen.

2 · Prioritize

Rank opportunities by likely value and risk so the first step is useful and manageable.

3 · Implement

Improve qualified workflows carefully, with clear review points and human accountability.

4 · Train

Give your team shared habits for prompting, data safety, and checking AI output.
