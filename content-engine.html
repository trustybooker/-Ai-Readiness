Seven useful AI questions for work and business.

What task repeats every week?

List the steps first. Then ask which parts AI could assist without removing necessary human judgment.

What information should stay private?

Know your workplace rules and the tool’s data settings before sharing customer, employee, financial, or confidential information.

Can you explain the output?

If you cannot explain or verify an AI-generated result, do not rely on it for an important decision.

Can you show your AI skill?

Build one useful work sample and be ready to explain the prompt, review process, corrections, and final result.

Where is work getting stuck?

Look for repeated writing, searching, summarizing, organizing, or follow-up work before buying another tool.

What needs a human decision?

Keep people responsible for money, legal commitments, sensitive customer communication, safety, and other high-impact actions.

What should you test first?

Choose a low-risk task, define what good looks like, test it, and compare the result before expanding.
