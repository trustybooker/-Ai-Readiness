Refunds, credits, and onboarding

Clear terms before money moves. Clear steps after it does.

AI Kollege only takes payment after a human review confirms the offer and fit. This page explains how refund and credit requests are handled, and exactly what happens after you buy.

The order of operations

Score → review → fit → payment → onboarding → delivery → proof

Payment links are only sent after the offer and scope are confirmed. That is deliberate: it keeps refund situations rare, because you know what you are buying before you buy it.

Refund and credit terms

How requests are handled.

  • Before work starts: if no training material has been delivered and no call, audit, or implementation work has been performed, you may request a refund or a credit toward another path.
  • After training material is delivered: requests are handled as a credit or partial refund where appropriate, reviewed case by case.
  • After a live call, audit, or implementation work: decisions depend on scope, time spent, and the written expectations agreed before work began.
  • How to ask: reply to your welcome email or use the booking form with your order details. A human reviews every request — there is no automated denial.

No blanket refund guarantee is advertised, and none should be assumed: each engagement has written scope, and refund decisions follow that scope. These terms may be updated before paid launch; the terms confirmed in your welcome email are the ones that apply to your purchase.

Buyer onboarding

What happens after you buy.

Every paid path follows the same simple sequence, so you always know the next step.

1 · Confirmation

You get a welcome message confirming the exact offer purchased, what is included, what is not, and your best contact method.

2 · Kickoff

Training paths get start instructions and a first proof-of-work assignment. Audits get a discovery call and agenda. Implementation begins only after written scope, boundaries, and human approval checkpoints are confirmed.

3 · Delivery and review

You build artifacts; a human reviews the work. Business engagements get the deliverables listed in their written scope.

4 · Handoff

You receive a summary of what was completed, your artifacts, what was not included, a next-step checklist, and the completion badge when proof-of-work was submitted.

Truth boundary

The same rules apply after payment.

  • No job, income, or revenue outcome is guaranteed at any price.
  • No accredited certification, license, degree, or compliance certification is issued.
  • Reviews and testimonials are only requested after real value was delivered, and never scripted.
  • High-ticket implementation starts only after a completed audit and written scope.
