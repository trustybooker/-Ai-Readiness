The order of operations

Score → path → checkout or review → onboarding → delivery → proof

Low-ticket individual passes can be purchased directly once their checkout links are tested. Higher-impact services stay review-first so fit, scope, access, and human approval points are clear before money moves.

Refund and credit terms

How requests are handled.

  • Before work starts: if no training material has been delivered and no call, audit, or implementation work has been performed, you may request a refund or a credit toward another path.
  • After training material is delivered: requests are handled as a credit or partial refund where appropriate, reviewed case by case.
  • After a live call, audit, or implementation work: decisions depend on scope, time spent, and the written expectations agreed before work began.
  • How to ask: reply to your welcome email or use the booking form with your order details. A human reviews every request — there is no automated denial.

No blanket refund guarantee is advertised, and none should be assumed. Self-serve training buyers receive the terms and onboarding steps tied to their pass; scoped business work follows the written engagement terms confirmed before work starts.

Buyer onboarding

What happens after you buy.

Every paid path follows a clear sequence, so you always know the next step.

1 · Confirmation

Stripe is the payment record for self-serve purchases. AI Kollege verifies the checkout email and exact pass before paid access or completion status is issued. Scoped services are confirmed against their written agreement.

2 · Kickoff

Training paths get start instructions and a first proof-of-work assignment. Audits get a discovery call and agenda. Implementation begins only after written scope, boundaries, and human approval checkpoints are confirmed.

3 · Delivery and review

You build artifacts; a human reviews the work when human review is part of the path. Business engagements get the deliverables listed in their written scope.

4 · Handoff

You receive a summary of what was completed, your artifacts, what was not included, a next-step checklist, and the completion badge when the badge requirements are met.

Truth boundary

The same rules apply after payment.

  • No job, income, or revenue outcome is guaranteed at any price.
  • No accredited certification, license, degree, or compliance certification is issued.
  • Reviews and testimonials are only requested after real value was delivered, and never scripted.
  • High-ticket implementation starts only after a completed audit and written scope.
  • Visiting the post-checkout page by itself is not proof of payment; the Stripe record is verified before paid fulfillment.
