AI readiness checklist

Mark each item Yes, Not yet, or Needs review.

1 · AI literacy

  • I can explain what AI can and cannot reliably do.
  • I verify important facts rather than trusting generated output automatically.
  • I can write a clear prompt with relevant context.
  • I know when a human decision is still required.

2 · Privacy and safety

  • I avoid putting passwords, financial credentials, medical details, or confidential client information into unapproved AI tools.
  • I know which workplace or business information is restricted.
  • I review AI output before sharing it externally.
  • I know when qualified legal, HR, clinical, financial, or other professional review is needed.

3 · Practical proof

  • I have at least one AI-assisted work sample I can explain honestly.
  • I can describe how I reviewed and improved the output.
  • I can explain where AI saved time or improved clarity without exaggerating results.
  • I can explain where human judgment changed the result.

4 · Workflow readiness

  • I can name one repeated task worth improving.
  • I understand the current process before trying to automate it.
  • I know the inputs, outputs, owner, likely failure points, and approval points.
  • I would test a small pilot before changing a critical process.

5 · Human responsibility

  • A named person remains accountable for important decisions.
  • Money, refunds, legal commitments, sensitive messages, and other high-impact actions receive human review.
  • There is a way to stop or correct an AI-assisted process.
  • People can tell when they are interacting with AI rather than a human.

Choose your next step

If several items need work, start with foundations. If work examples are the gap, consider the Job & Productivity Pass. If a business workflow or team is involved, a review can help you prioritize safely.
