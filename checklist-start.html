AI readiness checklist

Mark each item Yes, Not yet, or Needs review.

You do not need every answer to be “Yes.” The purpose is to see where training or human review will create the most value.

1 · AI literacy

  • I can explain what AI can and cannot reliably do.
  • I verify important facts instead of trusting generated output automatically.
  • I can write a clear problem-first prompt with useful context.
  • I know when a human decision is still required.

2 · Privacy and safety

  • I avoid entering passwords, financial credentials, medical details, or confidential client data into unapproved AI tools.
  • I know which business or workplace data is restricted.
  • I review AI output before sharing it externally.
  • I can identify when legal, HR, clinical, financial, or other qualified review is needed.

3 · Proof of skill

  • I have at least one AI-assisted work sample I can explain honestly.
  • I can describe my review process, not only the prompt I used.
  • I can show how AI improved time, clarity, quality, or consistency without exaggerating results.
  • I can explain where human judgment changed or corrected the output.

4 · Workflow readiness

  • I can name one repeated task worth improving.
  • I understand the current workflow before trying to automate it.
  • I know the inputs, outputs, owner, failure points, and approval points.
  • I would test a small pilot before changing a critical process.

5 · Human approval

  • A named person remains accountable for important decisions.
  • Money, refunds, legal commitments, sensitive messages, and high-impact actions have a human checkpoint.
  • There is a way to stop or correct an AI-assisted process.
  • People know when they are interacting with AI versus a human.

What to do next

If several items are “Not yet,” start with foundations. If job proof is the gap, use the Job & Productivity path. If a business workflow or team process is involved, request a human review before automation.
