Privacy and data handling

What we collect, how we use it, and how to get it removed.

This is a plain-language summary of how AI Kollege, operated by Fify Now LLC, handles the information you share. It describes our current practices; it is not legal advice, and a formal legal review is still in progress.

What we collect

Only what a human needs to help you.

  • Contact details you enter: name, email, and optional phone.
  • What you tell us: your problem/message, audience type, chosen path, timeline, and budget range.
  • Your free-score result and recommended path, when you complete the score.
  • Basic source data: the page you arrived on, referrer, and any UTM tags in the link.
  • If you use the AI assistant: the messages you send and its replies (the conversation transcript).
  • If you message us on WhatsApp: your WhatsApp number and the messages you send.

How we use it

To review your request and follow up.

  • Your submission is saved as a private lead record so a human can review it and recommend the safest useful next step.
  • When our lead tracker is unavailable, submissions are delivered to the operator by email instead, so your request is not lost.
  • The AI assistant sends your messages to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) to generate replies; anything involving money, refunds, or complex needs is handed to a human.
  • We use your information to respond, deliver what you request, and follow up. We do not sell it or share it with advertisers.

Your choices

Access, correction, and deletion.

You can ask us what we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it. Send the request through the request form or reply to any AI Kollege message, and a human will handle it.

  • Do not share sensitive personal, financial, health, or confidential business information in forms or the assistant.
  • The assistant is told not to request sensitive data; a human reviews anything sensitive.
  • We keep records only as long as needed for follow-up and legitimate business records.

Honesty note

What this page does not claim.

  • This summary is not legal advice and does not by itself certify compliance with any specific privacy law.
  • A formal privacy policy is under review and will replace this summary when finalized.
  • Questions? Use the request form and mark it as a privacy question.
