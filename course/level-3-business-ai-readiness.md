# Level 3: Business AI Readiness

Audience: small business owners, managers, team leads, and operators.

## Outcome

The business can identify useful AI opportunities, train people safely, and choose the first implementation without guessing.

## Module 1: Workflow discovery

Collect:

- What work repeats every week?
- What takes too long?
- What creates errors?
- What customers ask repeatedly?
- What documents or messages are reused?
- What reports are needed often?

Deliverable:

- Workflow discovery worksheet.

## Module 2: Opportunity scoring

Score each workflow from 1 to 5:

- Frequency.
- Time cost.
- Error risk.
- Business value.
- Information sensitivity.
- Human review need.

Good first projects are high frequency, useful, low-risk, and easy to review.

Deliverable:

- AI opportunity shortlist.

## Module 3: Risk snapshot

Review:

- What information is used.
- Who has access.
- What could go wrong.
- Where a human must review.
- What should stay manual.

Deliverable:

- Risk snapshot.

## Module 4: Human checkpoints

For each workflow, identify:

- What AI can draft.
- What AI can summarize.
- What AI can organize.
- What a human must approve.
- What AI should not decide alone.

Deliverable:

- Human approval checkpoint map.

## Module 5: Roadmap

Roadmap format:

- First workflow to improve.
- Training needed.
- Tools needed.
- People responsible.
- Test plan.
- Success metric.
- Review date.

Deliverable:

- 30-day AI readiness roadmap.

## Completion standard

Business submits:

- Workflow discovery worksheet.
- Opportunity shortlist.
- Risk snapshot.
- Approval checkpoint map.
- 30-day roadmap.

This prepares the business for a Team Training Sprint or Implementation Project.
