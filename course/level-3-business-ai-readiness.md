# Level 3: AI Kollege Business AI Readiness

Audience: small business owners, managers, team leads, and operators.

## Problem-first outcome

The business can identify useful AI opportunities, train people safely, choose the first implementation without guessing, and avoid buying tools before workflow clarity.

This level follows the Winning Skill: bigger problem, clearer buyer, smallest useful solution, proof, follow-up, and next larger problem.

## Module 1: Workflow discovery

Collect:

- What work repeats every week?
- What takes too long?
- What creates errors?
- What customers ask repeatedly?
- What quote, booking, payment, review, or follow-up steps leak value?
- What documents or messages are reused?
- What reports are needed often?

Deliverable:

- Workflow discovery worksheet.

## Module 2: Problem-size scoring

Score each workflow from 1 to 5:

- Frequency.
- Time cost.
- Error risk.
- Revenue or trust impact.
- Customer impact.
- Information sensitivity.
- Human review need.
- Ease of first fix.

Good first projects are frequent, costly, useful, low-risk, easy to review, and simple enough to improve quickly.

Deliverable:

- AI opportunity shortlist.

## Module 3: Risk and trust snapshot

Review:

- What information is used.
- Who has access.
- What could go wrong.
- Where a human must review.
- What should stay manual.
- What promise must not be made publicly.
- What customer trust could be damaged if AI is used badly.

Deliverable:

- Risk and trust snapshot.

## Module 4: Human checkpoints

For each workflow, identify:

- What AI can draft.
- What AI can summarize.
- What AI can organize.
- What a human must approve.
- What AI should not decide alone.
- What the fallback/manual process is.

Deliverable:

- Human approval checkpoint map.

## Module 5: Revenue and signal map

Teach:

AI should help the business solve visible leakage before expensive automation.

Map:

- Missed leads.
- Slow responses.
- Weak follow-up.
- Weak review requests.
- Repeated customer questions.
- Weak owner handoff.
- Content ideas from real customer questions.

Deliverable:

- Revenue and signal map.

## Module 6: Roadmap

Roadmap format:

- First workflow to improve.
- Problem it solves.
- Smallest useful fix.
- Training needed.
- Tools needed.
- People responsible.
- Test plan.
- Success metric.
- Proof to collect.
- Follow-up date.
- Next larger problem.

Deliverable:

- 30-day AI readiness roadmap.

## Completion standard

Business submits:

- Workflow discovery worksheet.
- Problem-size score.
- Risk and trust snapshot.
- Approval checkpoint map.
- Revenue and signal map.
- 30-day roadmap.

This prepares the business for a Team Training Sprint, Local Service Rescue Sprint, AI Readiness Lab, or scoped Implementation Project.
