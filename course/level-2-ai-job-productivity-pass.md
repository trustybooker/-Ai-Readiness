# Level 2: AI Kollege Job and Productivity Pass

Audience: job seekers, workers, admin, customer service, marketing, trades, operations, audio/content, and business support roles.

## Product promise

This pass is not a library of generic prompts or job-search tips. The learner builds a **personalized Job & Productivity Portfolio** from their real or target role: truthful career evidence, interview practice, reusable role workflows, a measured before/after productivity case study, and a proof-of-skill project.

## Problem-first outcome

The learner can explain practical AI skill truthfully, show proof-of-work, improve a repeated work process, review AI output responsibly, and communicate value without unsafe or exaggerated claims.

This level follows the Winning Skill: solve a real work problem, show proof, collect signal, and turn skill into a clear next step.

## Adaptive onboarding

Reuse Level 1/readiness data when available. Confirm only what changed:

- current or target role;
- job-search, advancement, productivity or business-support goal;
- one repeated task/workflow;
- relevant experience/evidence the learner can truthfully support;
- desired portfolio project.

Generate a concise role map identifying the most relevant modules, example family, likely proof opportunities and safety boundaries. Adapt examples and practice without lowering completion standards.

## Module experience

Each module follows **Goal → Role example → Guided build → Quality/safety check → Apply to real context → Save to portfolio**. Do not require the learner to start from a blank page when a scaffold, comparison or example is more usable.

## Module 1: Truthful AI resume evidence

Teach:

- Convert real experience into AI-assisted productivity examples.
- Never invent tools, responsibilities, metrics or outcomes.
- Avoid saying “AI expert” unless experience supports it.
- Use honest verbs: drafted, reviewed, organized, summarized, improved, automated with supervision.
- Show the problem solved and human contribution, not only the tool used.

Guided builder:

For each bullet capture **real task → problem → AI assistance → human review → supported result/evidence**. If a metric is unknown, do not fabricate one.

Deliverable:

- Three role-relevant AI-ready resume bullets with an evidence note for each.

## Module 2: Adaptive interview simulator

Teach the STAR-H format:

- Situation.
- Task.
- Action.
- Result.
- Human review.

Practice:

Use role-relevant questions and follow-ups. Feedback should flag vague claims, unsupported outcomes, missing human review, weak problem framing and opportunities to make the answer more concrete. Do not score confidence as factual competence.

Deliverable:

- At least two improved interview answers, including “How have you used AI to improve work?” and one role-specific scenario.

## Module 3: Role-based prompt and decision pack

Build prompts for the learner's actual or target role.

Example families:

- Admin/operations: notes, follow-up, scheduling preparation, SOP drafts.
- Customer service: response drafts, summaries and escalation notes.
- Healthcare support: administrative/communication support with privacy and human-review boundaries; no replacement of clinical judgment.
- Trades/field service: estimate explanations, customer communication, checklists and documentation support; no replacement of licensed/safety judgment.
- Marketing/content: research organization, campaign ideation, outlines and review checklists.
- Small business: intake, FAQ, follow-up, workflow documentation and decision preparation.
- Audio/creative: feedback organization, edit notes, release/checklist planning.

Each prompt includes input requirements, privacy boundary, expected output, verification step and when not to use it.

Deliverable:

- Five reusable role prompts plus one “do not automate / escalate” decision card.

## Module 4: Measured productivity workflow

Choose one repeated task and improve it.

Workflow map:

- Problem.
- Trigger.
- Input.
- Current/manual process.
- AI support.
- Human review.
- Final output.
- Failure/escalation path.
- Evidence that it helped.

Run at least one comparison where practical. Evidence may include time estimate, reduced steps, fewer revisions, improved completeness or another observable measure. Do not invent savings or productivity percentages.

Deliverable:

- Before/after workflow map plus a short evidence record describing what actually improved and what did not.

## Module 5: Proof-of-skill case study

Project choices adapt to the learner's role and goal:

- SOP/process improvement.
- Customer reply/escalation pack.
- Resume and interview evidence packet.
- Report-summary/review workflow.
- Content planning/review workflow.
- Trade/service communication workflow.
- Small-business intake/follow-up workflow.
- Creative production/review workflow.

Case-study structure:

**Problem → constraints → approach → AI role → human role → safety/verification → artifact → evidence → limitation → next iteration.**

Deliverable:

- One complete, portfolio-ready proof-of-skill case study with supporting artifact(s).

## Module 6: Opportunity signal without spam

Teach:

Skill becomes more valuable when the learner notices repeated problems, questions, objections or requests. Signal collection must not become manipulative outreach or spam.

Exercise:

Create three useful teaching messages/posts or professional explanations relevant to the learner's audience. Record real replies/questions when available; otherwise record expected objections explicitly as hypotheses, not actual customer evidence.

Deliverable:

- Three value-first communication artifacts.
- One signal log that clearly separates observed feedback from expected/hypothesized objections.

## Job & Productivity Portfolio

Completion assembles:

- role/goal map;
- three evidence-bounded resume bullets;
- improved interview answers;
- five role prompts and escalation card;
- before/after productivity workflow with evidence;
- proof-of-skill case study and artifact(s);
- communication artifacts and signal log;
- next-step development plan.

Where export/share is supported, provide a clean portable portfolio summary. The learner controls what personal information is included.

## Feedback and quality gates

Major artifacts are checked against explicit rubrics for truthfulness, usefulness, specificity, verification, privacy/safety and human contribution. AI feedback is assistance and must not be described as human review. Any promised human completion review must be performed by a human before being represented as such.

## Completion standard

Learner completes the portfolio components and required review steps. Completion is based on submitted work, not merely opening lessons.

Completion badge remains an **AI Kollege completion badge operated by Fify Now LLC**. It is not an accredited credential, job guarantee, income guarantee, official license, employer endorsement, or proof of professional competence beyond the reviewed course work.

## Ethical continuation

Do not automatically push every learner into another paid product. Recommend a business or implementation track only when the learner's stated goal or demonstrated work supports it. Explain the additional deliverable/value and give an appropriate free next action when paid help is unnecessary.
