# Level 2: AI Job and Productivity Pass

Audience: job seekers, workers, admin, customer service, marketing, trades, operations, audio/content, and business support roles.

## Outcome

The learner can explain practical AI skill, show proof-of-work, and use AI to improve daily productivity without making unsafe claims.

## Module 1: AI resume readiness

Teach:

- Convert real experience into AI-assisted productivity examples.
- Avoid saying “AI expert” unless experience supports it.
- Use honest verbs: drafted, reviewed, organized, summarized, improved, automated with supervision.

Exercise:

Write three resume bullets that connect existing experience with practical AI support.

Deliverable:

- Three AI-ready resume bullets.

## Module 2: AI interview readiness

Teach:

Use the STAR format:

- Situation.
- Task.
- Action.
- Result.
- Human review.

Exercise:

Prepare an answer to: “How have you used AI to improve work?”

Deliverable:

- One interview answer.

## Module 3: Role-based prompt packs

Teach:

Create prompts for the learner’s real role or target role.

Examples:

- Admin: summarize notes and draft follow-up.
- Customer service: draft polite responses and escalation notes.
- Trades: create estimate explanations or safety checklists.
- Marketing: draft campaign ideas and content outlines.
- Audio/content: organize feedback, edit notes, and quality checklists.

Deliverable:

- Five prompts for the learner’s role.

## Module 4: Productivity workflow

Teach:

Pick one repeated task and improve it.

Workflow map:

- Trigger.
- Input.
- AI support.
- Human review.
- Final output.

Deliverable:

- One before/after productivity workflow.

## Module 5: Proof-of-skill project

Project options:

- SOP draft.
- Customer reply pack.
- Resume and interview packet.
- Report summary workflow.
- Content planning workflow.

Deliverable:

- One complete project with explanation.

## Completion standard

Learner submits:

- Resume bullets.
- Interview answer.
- Role prompt pack.
- Productivity workflow.
- Proof-of-skill project.

Completion badge remains a Fify Now LLC completion badge, not an accredited credential.
