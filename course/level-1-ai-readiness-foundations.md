# Level 1: AI Readiness Foundations

Audience: beginners, workers, job seekers, and employees who need safe practical AI basics.

## Outcome

By the end, the learner can use AI for simple work tasks, check outputs, avoid unsafe data sharing, and create one proof-of-work artifact.

## Module 1: What AI can and cannot do

Teach:

- AI predicts and generates based on patterns.
- AI can be useful for drafting, summarizing, brainstorming, organizing, and role-play.
- AI can be wrong, outdated, biased, or overconfident.
- AI should not replace human judgment for sensitive decisions.

Exercise:

- Ask AI to explain a familiar topic.
- Identify three things that need verification.

Deliverable:

- One paragraph explaining what AI can and cannot safely do.

## Module 2: Prompt basics

Teach the prompt formula:

- Role.
- Goal.
- Context.
- Constraints.
- Output format.
- Review instruction.

Exercise:

Create one useful prompt for a daily task.

Deliverable:

- Starter prompt template.

## Module 3: Fact-checking and review

Teach:

- Do not trust important outputs blindly.
- Check names, numbers, dates, claims, policies, and instructions.
- Ask AI to list assumptions and uncertainty.
- Compare against trusted sources when needed.

Exercise:

- Review one AI answer and mark what must be checked.

Deliverable:

- Fact-check checklist.

## Module 4: Privacy and sensitive data

Teach:

Never paste sensitive information unless the tool and policy allow it:

- Customer private data.
- Employee private data.
- Financial information.
- Passwords or keys.
- Medical details.
- Legal documents.
- Business confidential data.

Exercise:

Rewrite a prompt to remove sensitive details.

Deliverable:

- Safe-use checklist.

## Module 5: Human judgment

Teach:

AI can support decisions but should not own decisions involving people, money, safety, compliance, or reputation.

Exercise:

Choose a task and mark what AI can draft and what a human must approve.

Deliverable:

- Human approval checkpoint map.

## Final proof project

Create one AI-assisted work sample:

- Email draft.
- SOP draft.
- Resume bullet improvement.
- Customer FAQ answer.
- Simple report summary.

Required reflection:

- What did AI help with?
- What did you review?
- What did you change?
- What should AI not decide alone?

## Completion standard

Complete all deliverables and submit proof project for human review. Completion badge language must say Fify Now LLC completion badge only, not accredited certification.
