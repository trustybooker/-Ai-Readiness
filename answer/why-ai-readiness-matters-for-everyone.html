People-first AI readiness

Why AI readiness matters for everyone

AI readiness matters because people need practical, safe, human-reviewed ways to use AI before they rely on it for work, business, privacy-sensitive tasks, or important decisions.

AI readiness is a life and work skill.

People do not need hype. They need clarity. AI readiness helps a person know what AI can help with, what must be checked, what information should stay private, and when a human should make the final decision.

It helps people avoid common mistakes

  • Using AI output without checking it.
  • Putting sensitive information into the wrong tool.
  • Buying software before understanding the workflow.
  • Claiming AI skill without proof-of-work.
  • Automating decisions that still need human review.
