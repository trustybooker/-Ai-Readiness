Safer AI use

Do not put sensitive information into an AI tool unless you are authorized and the tool is approved for it.

A useful default is simple: if information could harm a person, customer, employee, or business if exposed, stop and check the rules before entering it.

Before you paste

Need it? Allowed? Protected?

Use the minimum information necessary and remove identifying details when they are not needed.

Passwords and credentials

Do not enter passwords, API secrets, access tokens, PINs, recovery codes, or private authentication information.

Financial information

Avoid card numbers, bank credentials, tax identifiers, and other sensitive financial data unless an approved workflow specifically requires it.

Health and highly sensitive personal data

Do not paste medical records, diagnosis information, government identifiers, or similarly sensitive data into unapproved tools.

Customer and employee records

Follow your organization’s policy before sharing names, contact information, HR information, support histories, or private customer records.

Confidential business information

Protect contracts, unreleased plans, proprietary information, internal financials, and information covered by confidentiality obligations.

Anything you do not need

Even when a tool is approved, use the minimum amount of information needed to complete the task.

When you are unsure

Check your employer’s policy, the tool’s data settings, and any legal or contractual obligations that apply. For regulated or high-risk information, get qualified guidance rather than guessing.
