What should employees not put into AI tools?

Employees should not enter private, confidential, customer, employee, financial, legal, medical, security, password, or business-sensitive information into AI tools unless the company policy and tool settings clearly allow it. When unsure, remove identifying details or ask for approval first.

Use placeholders

Replace real names, account details, private numbers, and company secrets with neutral examples.

Ask first

If the data affects people, money, security, reputation, or compliance, get human approval first.

Train the team

A safe-use checklist prevents avoidable mistakes and gives employees confidence.
