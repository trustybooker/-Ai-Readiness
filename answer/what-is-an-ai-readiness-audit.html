Business AI readiness

An AI readiness audit helps you decide what is worth improving before you automate it.

The goal is to understand the current workflow, identify useful AI opportunities, surface risks, and prioritize a realistic next step.

Audit focus

Workflow → people → data → opportunity → plan

A good audit starts with the business problem, not a favorite AI tool.

Current workflows

Where does repeated work happen, where does it slow down, and who owns each decision?

People and skills

Who will use the system, what do they already know, and what training or review habits are needed?

Data and privacy

What information is involved, what is sensitive, and what tool or policy boundaries apply?

Useful opportunities

Which tasks are suitable for AI assistance and which should remain primarily human?

Risk and failure points

What could go wrong, how would it be detected, and what action should stop or correct the process?

Action plan

Prioritize one or two realistic improvements with clear owners, tests, and success measures.

When is an audit useful?

Use one when your business knows AI may help but does not yet know which process deserves attention first, or when several tools are being considered without a clear workflow plan.
