What is an AI readiness audit?

An AI readiness audit reviews how prepared a business is to use AI safely and productively. It looks at workflows, team skill, tool habits, information risk, automation opportunities, and human approval checkpoints before recommending training or implementation.

Audit outputs

A good audit produces a workflow map, opportunity shortlist, risk snapshot, approval checkpoint map, and a 30-day implementation roadmap.

When to buy one

  • Before buying AI tools.
  • Before training a team.
  • Before automating customer or internal workflows.
  • When staff are already using AI without clear rules.
