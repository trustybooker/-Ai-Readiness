How do you prove AI skills on a resume?

Prove AI skills by showing practical work samples, not vague claims. Use examples like a prompt workflow, improved SOP, customer response draft, reporting summary, resume improvement, or before-and-after productivity example, then explain how you reviewed the AI output for accuracy.

Show the workflow

Explain the task, prompt structure, output, review step, and final human-approved result.

Show the artifact

Use a real work sample: SOP, report, email sequence, checklist, resume bullet set, or project summary.

Show judgment

Employers trust AI skill more when you can explain limits, fact-checking, and what you would not let AI decide alone.

Good resume wording

Use honest verbs: drafted, reviewed, organized, summarized, improved, documented, checked, and implemented with human review.
