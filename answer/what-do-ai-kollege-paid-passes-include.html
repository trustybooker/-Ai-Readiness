What do AI Kollege paid passes actually include?

The paid value is guided, adaptive work that produces usable evidence—not access to generic AI facts you could find free online.

AI Starter Pass — $59 one time

Best for: people who need a dependable foundation for using AI safely and practically in their real role or goal.

You build: an AI boundary map, reusable prompt card, fact-check and correction evidence, privacy-safe prompt, human approval map, and a final proof project assembled into an AI Readiness Proof Pack.

How it works: the paid workspace verifies the purchase, adapts examples to the learner's context, saves progress in the browser, and requires actual artifacts plus quality checks before the final proof pack can be exported.

AI Job & Productivity Pass — $197 one time

Best for: job seekers and workers who want practical evidence of AI-assisted productivity they can explain truthfully.

You build: evidence-bounded resume bullets, improved interview answers, five reusable role prompts plus an escalation card, a measured before/after workflow, a proof-of-skill case study, and supporting work assembled into a Job & Productivity Portfolio.

How it works: role-aware examples and artifact-based progress focus on demonstrable work rather than passive lesson completion.

What makes the paid experience different?

Personalization, practice, verification, and proof.

  • Examples adapt to the learner's role and goal.
  • Progress is based on completed work, not page views.
  • Quality checks reinforce verification, privacy, and human judgment.
  • Saved artifacts become a portable proof pack or portfolio.
  • AI-generated feedback is never represented as human review.
  • No job, income, promotion, accreditation, or business result is guaranteed.
