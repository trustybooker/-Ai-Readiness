What is AI readiness?

AI readiness is the ability to use AI tools safely, productively, and responsibly. It includes AI literacy, privacy awareness, workflow judgment, practical prompting, human review habits, and a clear plan for how AI should support work without replacing human accountability.

Why it matters

People and teams can waste time, expose sensitive information, or make weak decisions when they use AI without training. Readiness gives them a safer starting point.

AI readiness includes

  • Knowing what AI can and cannot do.
  • Checking facts before acting.
  • Keeping sensitive details out of unsafe tools.
  • Mapping workflows before automating.
  • Using human approval for important decisions.
