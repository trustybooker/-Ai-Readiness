What the badge means

Completion, artifacts, and honest review status.

Work was submitted

Every badge means proof-of-work artifacts were submitted: checklists, prompt packs, workflow maps, work samples, and reflections tied to a real problem.

Human-reviewed when reviewed

A completion is marked human-reviewed only when a reviewer actually checked the artifacts and left reviewer notes. If it was not reviewed, it does not say it was.

Dated and versioned

Because AI changes fast, each issued badge includes the completion date and curriculum version, so the badge stays honest about when the training happened.

Truth boundary

What the badge is not.

  • Not accreditation and not an accredited certification.
  • Not an official license or licensed professional status.
  • Not a college degree, university credit, or school enrollment.
  • Not a job guarantee or employment eligibility claim.
  • Not an income or revenue guarantee.
  • Not a legal or compliance certification.

The badge proves completion of an AI Kollege training path, operated by Fify Now LLC, and submission of practical artifacts. Nothing else should be claimed from it.

What you can say

Honest language that still lands well.

Learners may say:

“I completed the AI Kollege Readiness Pass, operated by Fify Now LLC, and submitted practical AI proof-of-work artifacts for review.”

Learners should not say they are certified by an accredited AI authority, because AI Kollege makes no accreditation claim.

Verification status

No public verification page yet — on purpose.

AI Kollege does not currently offer a public badge verification page. Completion records are kept internally with the learner name, badge level, completion date, curriculum version, artifacts submitted, and reviewer notes when human-reviewed. A public verification page would only be added after unique record IDs and real verification logic exist — it is not promised until then.
