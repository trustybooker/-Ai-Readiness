What it represents

Completion backed by practical work.

Work completed

Learners complete the activities and practical outputs required for their selected AI Kollege path.

Review status is clear

When work receives a human review, that status can be recorded separately from basic completion.

Time and version matter

Completion can include the date and learning-path version so the record accurately reflects when the work was done.

What it is not

Completion recognition has clear limits.

  • It is not an accredited degree or academic credit.
  • It is not a professional license.
  • It is not a job, income, or business-result guarantee.
  • It is not a legal or regulatory compliance certification.

It simply documents AI Kollege work completed.

How to describe it

Keep your claim specific and accurate.

A clear description is: “Completed the AI Kollege [path name] and its practical AI work.”

You can then describe the actual projects, workflows, prompts, checklists, or work samples you created.
