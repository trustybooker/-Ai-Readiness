# AI Readiness Pass Business Strategy

## Core idea

AI Readiness Pass should not be positioned as another AI course. It should be positioned as a decision system:

> Score readiness, identify risk, choose the safest next step, and use human judgment before implementation.

This makes the business more believable, more useful, and easier to sell.

## Primary audiences

### 1. Job seekers and workers

Pain:
- They hear AI skills matter, but do not know what to learn.
- They cannot explain AI skill in interviews.
- Their resume has no proof of AI productivity.

Offer:
- AI Job & Productivity Pass.
- Resume examples.
- Interview talking points.
- Practical projects.
- Completion badge with truthful language.

### 2. Small businesses and local companies

Pain:
- Staff may be using AI without rules.
- Owners do not know which workflow to automate first.
- Buying tools before mapping the work wastes money.

Offer:
- Business AI Readiness Audit.
- Workflow map.
- Risk snapshot.
- Implementation roadmap.

### 3. Teams and organizations

Pain:
- Employees are unevenly prepared.
- AI usage can create privacy, quality, and brand risks.
- Leadership needs training plus operating rules.

Offer:
- Team Training Sprint.
- AI literacy training.
- Role examples.
- Human approval checkpoints.

## Conversion psychology

Use ethical persuasion only:

- Cost of inaction: falling behind, staff confusion, weak AI outputs, privacy mistakes, wasted software spend.
- Low-friction first step: free readiness score.
- Safety: human review before major decisions.
- Clarity: one score, one recommended path.
- Believability: no fake proof, no fake guarantee, no unsupported credential claims.
- Urgency: AI readiness matters now, but avoid fake countdowns or fake scarcity.

## Autonomous funnel

1. User lands on page.
2. Hero answers the 3-second test.
3. User takes readiness quiz.
4. Script recommends path.
5. Lead form sends score summary and selected path.
6. Fify Now LLC reviews and replies with next action.
7. Low-ticket buyers enter training.
8. Business leads enter audit or implementation review.

## Fulfillment notes

Minimum viable fulfillment should include:

- 30-minute human review call.
- Score interpretation.
- Skill gap summary.
- Recommended training path.
- One practical next-step checklist.

Business audit should include:

- Workflow interview.
- AI opportunity map.
- Risk and approval-point review.
- Tool shortlist.
- Implementation roadmap.

## Do not claim

- Accredited certification unless accreditation exists.
- Legal compliance.
- Guaranteed job placement.
- Guaranteed revenue.
- Existing customers or testimonials unless real and approved.
