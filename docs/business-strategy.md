# AI Readiness Pass Business Strategy

AI Readiness Pass is a decision system, not just an AI course.

## Core promise

Score readiness, identify the biggest gap, choose the safest next step, and use human judgment before implementation.

## Main audiences

### Job seekers and workers

They need practical AI proof for resumes, interviews, and daily work.

Offer:

- AI Job and Productivity Pass.
- Resume examples.
- Interview talking points.
- Practical projects.
- Completion badge with truthful language.

### Small businesses

They need workflow clarity before buying tools or automating.

Offer:

- Business AI Readiness Audit.
- Workflow map.
- Risk snapshot.
- Implementation roadmap.

### Teams

They need shared rules and practical examples.

Offer:

- Team Training Sprint.
- AI literacy training.
- Role examples.
- Human approval checkpoints.

## Conversion psychology

Use ethical persuasion:

- Cost of inaction: falling behind, staff confusion, weak AI outputs, privacy mistakes, wasted software spend.
- Low-friction first step: free readiness score.
- Safety: human review before major decisions.
- Clarity: one score, one recommended path.
- Believability: no fake proof, no fake guarantee, no unsupported credential claims.
- Urgency: AI readiness matters now, but avoid fake countdowns.

## Funnel

1. User lands on page.
2. Hero answers the 3-second test.
3. User takes readiness quiz.
4. Script recommends path.
5. Form tries the first-party tracker.
6. If configured, a GitHub Issue becomes the CRM record.
7. If not configured, email fallback protects the lead.
8. Human review determines next response.
9. Low-ticket buyers enter training.
10. Business leads enter audit or implementation review.

## Fulfillment notes

Minimum fulfillment:

- Human review call or written review.
- Score interpretation.
- Skill gap summary.
- Recommended training path.
- Practical next-step checklist.

Business audit:

- Workflow interview.
- AI opportunity map.
- Risk and approval-point review.
- Tool shortlist.
- Implementation roadmap.

## Do not claim

- Accredited certification unless accreditation exists.
- Legal compliance.
- Guaranteed job placement.
- Guaranteed revenue.
- Existing customers or testimonials unless real and approved.
