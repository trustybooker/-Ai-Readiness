# Business Implementation and Integration Playbook

Use this for business clients after the Business AI Readiness Audit.

## Rule before implementation

Do not implement random tools before the workflow is understood. Train first, map the workflow, identify risks, then implement the smallest useful system.

## Implementation phases

### Phase 1: Discovery

Collect:

- Business type.
- Team size.
- Current tools.
- Main workflow pain.
- Current manual steps.
- Sensitive data involved.
- Decision points that require human review.
- Success metric.

### Phase 2: Workflow map

Document:

- Trigger: what starts the workflow.
- Inputs: what information enters.
- Steps: what happens now.
- Outputs: what the business needs.
- Owner: who approves.
- Risks: what can go wrong.
- AI support: what AI can safely help with.
- Human checkpoint: what must be reviewed.

### Phase 3: Training

Train the team on:

- Safe AI use.
- Prompting for their roles.
- Fact-checking.
- Private data rules.
- Approval workflow.
- Examples based on their real work.

### Phase 4: Build or integrate

Only build what is scoped:

- Prompt library.
- SOP generator.
- Email or document workflow.
- Lead response workflow.
- Customer support draft workflow.
- Reporting summary workflow.
- CRM update workflow.
- Intake form and scoring workflow.

### Phase 5: Test

Test with:

- Realistic sample data.
- Edge cases.
- Human review.
- Role permissions.
- Failure handling.
- Before/after time comparison.

### Phase 6: Handoff

Deliver:

- Final workflow map.
- Tool instructions.
- Prompt library.
- Human approval checklist.
- Maintenance notes.
- Next improvement list.

## Deal structure

Use this order:

1. Paid audit.
2. Paid training sprint.
3. Paid implementation project.
4. Optional monthly support.

Avoid pure performance-based deals until baseline metrics and client responsibility are clear.

## What not to do

- Do not take over uncontrolled access.
- Do not promise automation can replace judgment.
- Do not connect sensitive systems without permission.
- Do not build without written scope.
- Do not keep maintaining unlimited custom work for a fixed small fee.

## Best implementation targets

Good first targets:

- Lead intake and response.
- Customer FAQ drafts.
- SOP creation.
- Estimate/proposal drafts.
- Review request workflows.
- Internal training summaries.
- Weekly reporting summaries.
- Email follow-up drafts.

Riskier targets:

- Legal advice.
- Medical advice.
- Credit/financial decisions.
- Hiring decisions.
- Employee discipline.
- Anything using sensitive personal data without clear policy.
