# Google Flow / Veo Realism Video Prompts

Use these prompts to create realistic marketing clips for AI Readiness Pass. Do not use fake testimonials, fake client logos, fake certifications, or invented proof.

## Main hero video, 16:9, 8-12 seconds

Create a fully realistic cinematic website hero video for "AI Readiness Pass by Fify Now LLC."

Scene: A real small business owner and a real job seeker sit in a clean modern office, reviewing an AI readiness score on a laptop with a human consultant. The consultant is calm, trustworthy, practical, and professional. The feeling is not futuristic sci-fi; it should feel like a real business training session that could happen today.

Visual style: documentary realism, natural light, real skin texture, subtle film grain, realistic camera movement, no plastic faces, no over-smoothed skin, no fake corporate stock-photo look, no floating holograms, no sci-fi blue nonsense. Use shallow depth of field, authentic facial expressions, natural imperfections, realistic hands, realistic eyes, realistic teeth, realistic clothing fabric, realistic office background.

Mood: trustworthy, urgent but calm, practical, high-converting, human-guided, credible. The viewer should immediately feel: "I need to know how AI-ready I am before I fall behind."

Camera: 35mm documentary lens, slow push-in from the laptop screen to the people’s faces, then a close-up of the consultant pointing to a simple readiness dashboard. Smooth handheld realism, not robotic. Natural office ambience.

On-screen text, clean and minimal:
- Find out how AI-ready you are.
- Get your score.
- Human review. Practical training. Safer implementation.
- AI Readiness Pass — Fify Now LLC

Dialogue option:
Consultant: "Your score is a starting point. Now we decide the safest next move."
Business owner: "So this shows what my team needs before we automate?"
Consultant: "Exactly. Train first, then implement with human checkpoints."

Lip sync rule: If dialogue is used, lips, body movement, facial expressions, hands, and breathing must stay synchronized.

Avoid: AI artifacts, distorted hands, extra fingers, warped laptop screens, fake text, unreadable dashboard text, robotic expressions, mismatched lip sync, plastic skin, exaggerated smiles, unrealistic lighting, fantasy visuals, sci-fi holograms, fake testimonials, fake logos, fake certification claims.

## Quality control checklist

Reject the video if:
- Hands look wrong.
- The laptop screen text is unreadable or fake-looking.
- The scene feels like a stock ad instead of a real consultation.
- Lip sync is off.
- People look plastic or overly smooth.
- It claims guaranteed results.
- It implies official accreditation or legal compliance.
