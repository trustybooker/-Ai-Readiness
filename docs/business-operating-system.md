# AI Readiness Pass Business Operating System

This is the working system for running AI Readiness Pass consistently.

## Business promise

Fify Now LLC helps people and businesses understand their AI readiness, build practical skills, and implement AI with human judgment and safe workflows.

## Core workflow

1. Lead takes free score.
2. First-party tracker creates a lead issue.
3. Operator reviews score, tier, budget, timeline, and message.
4. Operator sends response script.
5. Lead chooses training, audit, sprint, or implementation review.
6. Payment link or booking link is sent only after fit is clear.
7. Client enters onboarding.
8. Training or audit is delivered.
9. Client receives deliverables and offboarding.
10. Follow-up offer is made if appropriate.

## Offer handling

### Free AI Readiness Score

Purpose: lead capture and triage.

Deliverable:

- Score interpretation.
- Recommended next path.
- One next-step suggestion.

### AI Starter Pass

Purpose: beginner training.

Deliverable:

- Safe AI use checklist.
- Prompt basics.
- Fact-checking habit guide.
- One practical proof project.

### AI Job and Productivity Pass

Purpose: help workers and job seekers prove AI skill.

Deliverable:

- Resume bullet examples.
- Interview talking points.
- Role-based prompt pack.
- Proof-of-skill project.

### Business AI Readiness Audit

Purpose: decide what the business should train or implement first.

Deliverable:

- Workflow discovery summary.
- AI opportunity map.
- Risk and approval checkpoint map.
- Implementation roadmap.

### Team Training Sprint

Purpose: train a group and create shared safe-use rules.

Deliverable:

- Live training session.
- Role-based examples.
- Team safe-use checklist.
- Team next-step roadmap.

### AI Implementation Project

Purpose: guided execution after audit.

Deliverable:

- Scoped implementation plan.
- Tool stack recommendation.
- Human approval checkpoints.
- Testing plan.
- Handoff document.

## Weekly operating rhythm

Monday:

- Review all new leads.
- Prioritize hot and audit-ready leads.
- Send follow-ups.

Tuesday to Thursday:

- Run calls, training, audits, and implementation work.
- Update issue status after every client touch.

Friday:

- Review outcomes.
- Update answer hub and course material from real questions.
- Improve one workflow or template.

## Quality rules

- Do not overbuild before payment.
- Do not promise results outside control.
- Do not call anything accredited unless it is accredited.
- Keep every client action documented.
- Convert repeated answers into training assets.
- Convert repeated workflow problems into templates.

## Human review rule

AI can score and organize. A human must decide the recommendation, especially for business, legal, financial, customer, employee, or sensitive workflow decisions.
