# Two-Layer Growth System

AI Readiness Pass now has two connected layers.

## Layer 1: Conversion Page

Purpose: convert visitors quickly.

Path:

1. Hero explains the problem in three seconds.
2. Visitor starts the free AI readiness score.
3. Quiz creates score, path, and lead tier.
4. Visitor requests human review.
5. First-party tracker creates a lead record.
6. Email fallback protects the lead if tracker is not configured.

Primary page:

- `index.html`

## Layer 2: Answer Hub

Purpose: become a useful direct-answer asset for search, AI answer engines, and long-tail buyer questions.

Pages:

- `answers.html`
- `answer/what-is-ai-readiness.html`
- `answer/how-to-prove-ai-skills-on-resume.html`
- `answer/why-train-before-ai-automation.html`
- `answer/what-is-an-ai-readiness-audit.html`
- `answer/what-not-to-put-into-ai-tools.html`
- `answer/how-small-businesses-start-ai-safely.html`

Each answer page should:

- Give a direct answer near the top.
- Avoid fake claims.
- Give practical examples.
- Link back to the score, checklist, Lab, or review form.

## Lead magnet

Page:

- `checklist.html`

Purpose:

- Convert visitors who are not ready to book.
- Capture first-party leads.
- Route people into the score, training, or Lab.

## Recurring value layer

Page:

- `lab.html`

Purpose:

- Build waitlist demand for AI Readiness Lab.
- Create recurring value through office hours, workflow templates, tool updates, and human review.
- Avoid turning the company into unlimited custom development.

## Operating rule

Answers attract attention. The checklist captures low-friction demand. The score qualifies intent. Human review decides the safest offer. Training, audit, or implementation creates revenue.
