# Imagery System

Goal: make AI Readiness Pass look credible, human, practical, and premium without fake proof or AI-looking visuals.

## Visual rule

Do not use random AI stock-style people. The imagery must either be:

1. Real approved photography/video, or
2. Clean branded graphics that do not pretend to be real people.

## Best hero setup

Use one of these:

- Approved real hero video: consultant, job seeker, business owner, laptop readiness score.
- Approved real still image from that video as fallback poster.
- Branded abstract social preview while real footage is pending.

Current safe asset:

- `assets/og-ai-readiness-pass.svg` — branded social preview, not fake realism.

## Hero video quality standard

Accept only if:

- Hands, eyes, teeth, and faces look real.
- Laptop screen is not warped.
- Text overlays are readable.
- Skin has natural texture.
- Body movement is natural.
- If speaking, lip sync is accurate.
- Scene looks like a real consultation, not sci-fi.

Reject if:

- It has plastic skin.
- It looks like fake stock media.
- It uses floating holograms.
- It invents testimonials, logos, awards, or certifications.
- It implies guaranteed job or revenue outcomes.

## Recommended image set

Create these final assets before launch:

- `assets/hero-readiness-consultation.mp4` — 16:9, 8 to 12 seconds.
- `assets/hero-readiness-poster.webp` — poster still from approved video.
- `assets/social-square.webp` — 1:1 social preview.
- `assets/social-vertical.webp` — 9:16 ad creative.
- `assets/og-ai-readiness-pass.webp` — Open Graph preview image.

## SEO image requirements

- Use descriptive filenames.
- Compress for web.
- Add width and height when embedded.
- Use accurate alt text.
- Do not keyword-stuff alt text.
