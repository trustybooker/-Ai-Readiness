# Onboarding, Offboarding, Refunds, and Client Handoff

This document gives Fify Now LLC a consistent client experience.

## Onboarding principles

- Keep it simple.
- Confirm the exact offer purchased.
- Confirm expectations before work starts.
- Collect only information needed to deliver the service.
- Do not begin custom implementation until scope and payment are clear.

## Onboarding checklist: individual training

1. Confirm offer path.
2. Confirm email and best contact method.
3. Send welcome message.
4. Send training start instructions.
5. Ask for role, goal, and current AI tool comfort level.
6. Assign first proof-of-work artifact.
7. Set review date or submission deadline.

## Onboarding checklist: business audit

1. Confirm business name and decision maker.
2. Confirm workflow to review.
3. Ask for current tools used.
4. Ask for team size and roles.
5. Ask what success means in 30 to 60 days.
6. Schedule discovery call.
7. Send audit agenda.
8. Create GitHub issue status: scheduled.

## Onboarding checklist: implementation project

1. Complete business audit first.
2. Confirm scope in writing.
3. Confirm what is included and excluded.
4. Confirm access boundaries.
5. Confirm human approval points.
6. Confirm timeline.
7. Confirm payment milestone.
8. Begin only after all items are clear.

## Offboarding checklist

1. Summarize what was completed.
2. List deliverables.
3. List what the client should do next.
4. List what was not included.
5. Provide maintenance or next-level offer if appropriate.
6. Ask for feedback.
7. Ask for a truthful review only after real value was delivered.
8. Mark lead/client issue completed.

## Refund and credit policy placeholder

Final policy must be reviewed before paid launch. Until then, use this conservative operating rule:

- If no work has started, offer refund or credit.
- If training material has been delivered, offer credit or partial refund only if appropriate.
- If a live call, audit, or implementation work has been performed, refund decisions should depend on scope, time spent, and written expectations.
- Do not promise refunds on the site until the final policy is approved.

## Client handoff package

For every paid client, provide the simplest useful handoff:

- Score or audit summary.
- Recommended path.
- Completed artifacts.
- Next-step checklist.
- Risks or warnings.
- Support or follow-up option.

## Review request rule

Only request a review after the client has received real value. Never ask for fake, exaggerated, or scripted reviews.
