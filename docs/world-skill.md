# The World Skill

## Name

The World Skill

## Purpose

Use this skill to build AI Kollege and every related business asset around truth, service, Jesus-centered integrity, customer transformation, disciplined revenue, love, loyalty, and long-term trust.

This skill does not promise financial success. It creates the operating standard most likely to produce strong financial results: clear value, real proof, excellent delivery, retention, referrals, and ethical sales.

## Core declaration

Build what helps people. Tell the truth. Deliver more value than expected. Keep human dignity first. Use AI as a servant, not a substitute for wisdom, love, or accountability.

## Jesus-centered business standard

Every decision must pass these filters:

1. Truth: Is the claim honest, verified, and not exaggerated?
2. Love: Does this protect and help the person, not exploit them?
3. Service: Does the offer solve a real problem with practical value?
4. Stewardship: Does this use time, money, tools, data, and attention wisely?
5. Justice: Is the pricing, promise, refund logic, and delivery fair?
6. Peace: Does the customer feel clearer and safer after engaging?
7. Fruit: Does the system produce trust, competence, loyalty, referrals, and repeat value?

## Non-negotiables

Do not use:

- fake scarcity,
- fake testimonials,
- fake income claims,
- fake credentials,
- spiritual manipulation,
- pressure against poor or desperate people,
- fear-based selling,
- hidden fees,
- vague deliverables,
- unsupported compliance claims,
- job or revenue guarantees.

Do use:

- plain truth,
- proof-of-work,
- human review,
- written scope,
- clear pricing,
- useful free value,
- easy next steps,
- customer dignity,
- honest follow-up,
- refunds or corrections when delivery falls short.

## The World Skill business formula

1. Attract with useful truth.
2. Diagnose with a simple score or checklist.
3. Recommend only the next right step.
4. Deliver a visible result quickly.
5. Capture proof ethically.
6. Follow up with care.
7. Turn satisfied customers into referrals, testimonials, repeat buyers, and community members.
8. Improve the offer every week from real customer feedback.

## Offer ladder

1. Free AI Readiness Score
   - Purpose: diagnose and build trust.
   - Success measure: user understands their biggest gap.

2. AI Readiness Checklist
   - Purpose: useful free guide and lead capture.
   - Success measure: user can identify privacy, skill, job, or workflow risks.

3. AI Starter Pass
   - Purpose: foundation training.
   - Success measure: user can use AI safely for basic work tasks.

4. AI Job and Productivity Pass
   - Purpose: job-market proof.
   - Success measure: user has explainable examples, resume language, and work samples.

5. Business AI Readiness Audit
   - Purpose: business diagnosis.
   - Success measure: owner has workflow map, risk snapshot, opportunity shortlist, and 30-day roadmap.

6. Team Training Sprint
   - Purpose: team alignment.
   - Success measure: team has shared rules, examples, review habits, and safe-use policy draft.

7. AI Implementation Partner Review
   - Purpose: qualify scoped implementation work.
   - Success measure: written scope, risks, timeline, deliverables, and approval checkpoints.

8. AI Readiness Lab
   - Purpose: community, retention, updates, office hours, templates, and loyalty.
   - Success measure: members keep learning and keep returning because it helps them.

## Loyalty engine

Love and loyalty come from a repeated pattern:

1. Make the customer feel seen.
2. Diagnose the real problem.
3. Give one useful win before asking for more.
4. Explain what you will do and what you will not do.
5. Deliver quickly.
6. Follow up.
7. Celebrate progress.
8. Invite honest feedback.
9. Improve the system.
10. Keep promises small enough to fulfill and valuable enough to matter.

## Boss Skill use

When using Boss Skill with World Skill:

1. Audit the asset.
2. Identify what is not aligned with truth, love, value, loyalty, and revenue.
3. Remove internal language from public pages.
4. Remove fake claims and unclear promises.
5. Strengthen the offer ladder.
6. Make the next action obvious.
7. Make proof easier to collect.
8. Make customer delivery easier to fulfill.
9. Add follow-up and referral loops.
10. Validate before calling it ready.

## Prompt to use

Use this prompt whenever you want the World Skill applied:

"Use the World Skill and Boss Skill on this business asset. Audit it for Jesus-centered integrity, truth, customer love, loyalty, offer clarity, revenue logic, liability safety, delivery proof, follow-up, referrals, and long-term trust. Remove anything fake, confusing, manipulative, weak, or internally focused. Make the next best action clear. Do not promise guaranteed money, jobs, legal compliance, or accreditation. Package the result so it can create real value, repeat customers, referrals, and ethical financial growth."

## Final standard

The business should make people say:

- This helped me.
- This told me the truth.
- This made AI feel safer and clearer.
- This respected my situation.
- This gave me a next step I can actually use.
- I would trust this again.
- I would tell someone else about it.

That is the root of love, loyalty, referrals, and durable financial growth.
