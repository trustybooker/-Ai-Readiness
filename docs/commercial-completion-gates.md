# AI Kollege Commercial Completion Gates

AI Kollege is commercially complete only when all six capabilities below are proven in production. A green build alone is not enough.

1. Acquisition — SEO/AEO, strong landing pages, lead capture and measurable conversion events.
2. Conversion — clear truthful offers/pricing, verified checkout, abandoned-signup recovery and frictionless enrollment.
3. Delivery — complete high-quality curriculum, progress tracking, assessments, completion rules, promised recognition and a strong learner workspace.
4. Communication — working Resend transactional email, onboarding, reminders, completion messages, support and appropriate lifecycle email.
5. Operations — Owner Studio can manage learners, access, payments, communications and important business events without routine engineering intervention.
6. Measurement — trace traffic → lead → checkout → purchase → activation → course progress → completion → retention/revenue.

Cross-cutting requirements apply to every gate:

- Security/reliability: authorization boundaries, owner isolation, webhook verification, idempotency, rate limiting, spam resistance, secret handling, payment/access integrity, graceful third-party failure, recovery and no public PII leakage.
- Truth/value: every public product claim must map to an implemented customer outcome. Do not treat a button click as a conversion, automated review as human review, course completion as accreditation/licensure, or an unimplemented capability as live.

The final production certification must record evidence for each gate and clearly mark anything not proven as NOT COMPLETE.
