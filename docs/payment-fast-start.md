# Payment Fast Start

## Best immediate setup

Use **PayPal Payment Links first** if speed is the priority, with **Square Payment Links as the backup**. Keep **Stripe Payment Links** as the clean long-term checkout and subscription setup.

## Why PayPal first

PayPal Payment Links can be created without custom checkout code and can be sent by email, text, social media, or messaging apps. Use it for the first sales while Stripe is being verified.

Recommended PayPal links to create:

1. AI Starter Pass — $59
2. AI Job & Productivity Pass — $197
3. Business AI Readiness Audit — $497
4. Team Training Sprint deposit — choose a deposit amount before launch

## Why Square second

Square Payment Links are also fast and strong for service businesses. Square supports payment links, buy buttons, QR codes, and common customer payment methods. Use Square if PayPal setup is delayed or if you want Square Dashboard reporting.

Recommended Square links mirror the PayPal links:

1. AI Starter Pass — $59
2. AI Job & Productivity Pass — $197
3. Business AI Readiness Audit — $497
4. Team Training Sprint deposit

## Stripe long-term setup

Stripe should still be connected because it is strong for:

- Payment links
- Checkout pages
- Subscriptions
- Receipts
- Refunds
- Future automation
- CRM and fulfillment workflows

## Button replacement plan

Do not add live pay buttons until a real test purchase works.

When links are ready, replace these buttons:

- `AI Starter Pass` → PayPal/Square/Stripe $59 link
- `AI Job & Productivity Pass` → PayPal/Square/Stripe $197 link
- `Business AI Readiness Audit` → PayPal/Square/Stripe $497 link

High-ticket offers should keep the application/review flow instead of instant checkout until the sales process is proven.

## Safety rules

- Add a refund or credit policy before taking payments.
- Do not promise job placement, revenue, legal compliance, or accredited certification.
- Keep receipts and customer records in the payment dashboard.
- Confirm each link sends payment to the correct Fify Now LLC account before publishing.
