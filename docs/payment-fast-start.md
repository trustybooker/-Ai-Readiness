# Payment Fast Start

Create and test payment links before using them on the live site.

## Order

1. Square first for simple service payments and deposits.
2. PayPal as backup.
3. Stripe for the long-term checkout and subscription system.

## Offers

- AI Starter Pass.
- AI Job and Productivity Pass.
- Business AI Readiness Audit.
- Team Training Sprint deposit.
- AI Implementation Review deposit.

## Before launch

Confirm the offer name, price, deliverables, limits, refund rules, timeline, and onboarding steps.

## After payment

Update the lead record, mark payment status, send onboarding steps, assign the first deliverable, schedule the session if included, track completion, and offboard properly.

## Rules

Do not use untested links. Do not promise instant credentials. Do not promise job or revenue results. Do not start implementation work without written scope.
