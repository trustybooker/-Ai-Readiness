# Temporary Lead Inbox

Use `fifynow@gmail.com` as the temporary lead inbox until the domain inbox is fully verified.

Recommended immediate path:

1. Deploy the site on Netlify.
2. Use Netlify Forms to capture submissions.
3. In Netlify, enable form notification emails to `fifynow@gmail.com`.
4. Submit one test lead from the deployed site.
5. Confirm the lead appears in Netlify Forms and in Gmail.
6. After that works, connect the permanent domain email and CRM.

Do not claim email automation is fully verified until the deployed form has received and delivered a real test lead.
