# AI Kollege Brand Launch Plan

## Brand decision

AI Kollege is the public-facing brand.

Fify Now LLC remains the operating/legal business behind the brand unless a separate entity is formed later.

Recommended public wording:

- `AI Kollege`
- `AI Kollege Readiness Pass`
- `AI Readiness Pass by AI Kollege`
- `Operated by Fify Now LLC`

Avoid public wording that makes AI Kollege sound like an accredited college, university, school, or licensed institution.

## Domain

Primary future domain:

`aikollege.com`

Use the Netlify URL for testing until DNS is connected and verified:

`https://ai-readiness-pass.netlify.app`

After the domain is connected, update canonical URLs, sitemap URLs, Open Graph URLs, and robots sitemap reference to the final AI Kollege domain.

## Positioning

AI Kollege helps people and businesses become practically AI-ready before they waste money, expose private information, automate the wrong workflow, or claim AI skills they cannot prove.

Core promise:

`Score your AI readiness, find your biggest gap, and choose the safest useful next step.`

## Offer ladder

1. Free AI Readiness Score
2. AI Starter Pass
3. AI Job & Productivity Pass
4. Business AI Readiness Audit
5. Team Training Sprint
6. AI Implementation Partner Review
7. AI Readiness Lab interest list

## Safety rules

Do not claim:

- accredited college status,
- official certification unless legally/operationally supported,
- guaranteed employment,
- guaranteed revenue,
- legal compliance advice,
- automated decisions without human review.

Use:

- `completion badge`, not `accredited certificate`, unless accreditation is obtained.
- `training`, `readiness`, `proof-of-work`, and `human review`.
- `operated by Fify Now LLC` for legal clarity.

## Namecheap to Netlify connection checklist

When ready to connect `aikollege.com`:

1. Add `aikollege.com` in Netlify site domains.
2. Choose whether Netlify DNS or Namecheap DNS will manage records.
3. If keeping Namecheap DNS, add the Netlify-provided DNS records exactly as Netlify shows them.
4. Wait for DNS propagation.
5. Enable HTTPS in Netlify after the domain verifies.
6. Test:
   - `https://aikollege.com`
   - `https://www.aikollege.com`
   - lead form submission
   - Google Calendar booking link
   - sitemap and robots
7. After successful domain verification, update canonical URLs and sitemap URLs from the test/legacy domain to `https://aikollege.com`.

## Launch rule

Do not switch SEO canonicals to `aikollege.com` until the domain resolves, HTTPS is active, and the live user flow passes testing.
