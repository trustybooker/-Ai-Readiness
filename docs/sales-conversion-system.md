# Sales Conversion System

## Sales principle

AI Readiness Pass should sell by helping people clearly see their next best step. The sale should feel like direction, not pressure.

The correct sales promise is:

**Know where you stand, fix the most important gap, build proof, and use AI with human judgment.**

## Conversion path

1. Visitor arrives from search, social, referral, or direct link.
2. Hero answers the 3-second question: why this matters now.
3. Visitor takes the free readiness score or reads an answer page.
4. The score or page routes the visitor to a practical next step.
5. The form captures contact, path, score, timeline, budget, and message.
6. Lead tracker creates a review record.
7. Follow-up recommends the safest useful paid or free step.
8. Paid buyer enters training, audit, sprint, implementation review, or lab membership.

## Offer-fit matrix

| Visitor need | Best first offer | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curious or unsure | Free score or checklist | Reduces friction and builds trust. |
| Beginner | AI Starter Pass | Small step, low risk, practical foundation. |
| Job seeker or worker | AI Job & Productivity Pass | Converts AI interest into proof-of-work. |
| Small business owner | Business AI Readiness Audit | Prevents buying tools before workflow clarity. |
| Team or organization | Team AI Training Sprint | Creates shared rules and safe habits. |
| Qualified business with clear workflow | Implementation Review | Only after fit, scope, and risk are clear. |
| Returning learner or client | AI Readiness Lab | Recurring value and ongoing updates. |

## Sales optimization rules

- Put the free score first because it creates trust and first-party demand.
- Keep the first paid offer simple so people can start without fear.
- Use the business audit before implementation so the expensive offer feels earned.
- Use the team sprint for organizations that need shared behavior change.
- Use the lab for retention, updates, and recurring value.
- Do not send every lead to the highest-priced offer.
- Do not add payment links until each checkout link is tested.
- Do not promise jobs, income, legal compliance, accreditation, or guaranteed business results.

## Follow-up sequence

### First reply

Thank the person, summarize their goal, name their likely gap, and recommend one next step.

### If they are unsure

Send the free checklist or score first.

### If they completed the score

Explain what the score suggests and what to do next.

### If they are a job seeker

Recommend AI Job & Productivity Pass if they need resume/interview proof.

### If they are a business

Recommend Business AI Readiness Audit before implementation.

### If they are a team

Recommend Team AI Training Sprint.

### If they ask for implementation

Confirm scope, budget, access boundaries, and human approval points before accepting.

## Objection handling

- **I am not technical:** Start with the free score or AI Starter Pass.
- **I need this for jobs:** Build proof-of-work, not vague AI claims.
- **My business needs AI:** Audit one workflow before buying tools.
- **Can AI do everything for me:** No. AI can support work, but humans must review important decisions.
- **Is this certified:** It is a Fify Now LLC completion badge, not accredited unless accreditation is obtained later.
- **Why pay:** Paid paths add structure, review, artifacts, and a clearer next step.

## Conversion metrics

Track:

- free score starts,
- free score completions,
- form submissions,
- booking clicks,
- checklist downloads,
- lab waitlist signups,
- payment-link requests,
- booked reviews,
- audit requests,
- paid conversions,
- follow-up replies,
- refund or confusion causes.

## Sales quality standard

A sale is high quality only if the buyer understands:

- what they are buying,
- what is included,
- what is not included,
- what proof or deliverable they will receive,
- what claims are not being made,
- what the next step is after purchase.

## Human-first close

The strongest close is not pressure. It is clarity:

“Based on what you shared, the safest useful next step is this path. It gives you the proof, structure, and review you need without jumping into a larger offer before you are ready.”
