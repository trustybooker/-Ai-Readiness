# Human Value Standard

AI Readiness Pass should help people make better AI decisions before they buy tools, courses, audits, or implementation services.

## Core belief

AI readiness should protect and empower people first.

## Public promise

Fify Now LLC should help visitors understand:

- what AI can help with,
- what AI should not decide alone,
- what information should stay private,
- what skills they need next,
- what workflow is safe to improve first,
- when a human reviewer is needed,
- and when a free checklist is enough for now.

## People-first rules

1. Help before selling.
2. Recommend the safest useful next step, not automatically the most expensive step.
3. Keep claims truthful and plain.
4. Avoid fake urgency, fake proof, fake credentials, and fake scarcity.
5. Protect sensitive data and privacy.
6. Keep humans responsible for important decisions.
7. Build practical proof-of-work, not empty AI hype.
8. Support beginners clearly and respectfully.
9. Give businesses a clear path before automation.
10. Refresh the system as AI changes.

## Visitor outcomes

A visitor should leave with at least one useful outcome even without buying:

- a clearer understanding of AI readiness,
- a readiness score,
- a checklist,
- a safer prompt habit,
- a warning about sensitive data,
- a better first workflow idea,
- or a clearer next step for work, business, or training.

## Offer ethics

The offer ladder should stay aligned with actual need:

- Free checklist or score for people who only need clarity.
- Starter training for beginners.
- Job/productivity path for people who need proof-of-work.
- Business audit for teams that need workflow clarity.
- Team sprint for shared rules and training.
- Implementation only after scope, risk, and human approval points are understood.
- Lab membership for ongoing updates and practical improvement.

## Follow-up standard

Every lead should be handled as a person asking for direction, not just as a buyer. Follow-up should explain the safest next step, the limits of the offer, and the reason for the recommendation.

## Future direction

The long-term goal is a practical AI readiness operating system for people and small organizations:

- readiness scoring,
- proof-of-work learning,
- privacy and safety habits,
- human review standards,
- workflow mapping,
- honest badges,
- and guided implementation when appropriate.
