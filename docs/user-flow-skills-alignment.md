# AI Kollege Skills-Aligned User Flow

## Purpose

This document aligns the AI Kollege app, courses, completion badge, lead capture, daily content engine, and business model with the Winning Skill, World Skill, Boss Skill, and problem-first operating law.

## Core flow

1. Visitor arrives from content, search, referral, or direct link.
2. Visitor sees the real problem: AI confusion, skill gap, privacy risk, workflow waste, or team risk.
3. Visitor chooses one low-friction action:
   - take the free AI Readiness Score,
   - request the checklist,
   - read an answer,
   - submit audience signal,
   - request human review,
   - book a review.
4. The app captures the problem, audience type, path, timeline, budget range, source, and message.
5. Human review recommends the smallest useful next step.
6. User enters the right path:
   - Level 1 Foundations,
   - Level 2 Job and Productivity,
   - Level 3 Business AI Readiness,
   - Level 4 Implementation Partner Track,
   - AI Kollege Lab,
   - Local Service Rescue Sprint.
7. User produces proof-of-work artifacts.
8. Completion is reviewed using the safe badge standard.
9. User is invited to the next useful problem-solving step.
10. Repeated signals from users become future posts, products, templates, or Lab topics.

## Skill alignment

### Winning Skill

- Problem first.
- Smallest useful next step.
- Manual help before software.
- Daily content and audience signal.
- Proof before productization.

### World Skill

- Truth without hype.
- Service before money.
- Human dignity.
- No pressure tactics.
- Fruit over noise.

### Boss Skill

- Prioritize by problem size and urgency.
- Avoid scattered building.
- Turn user signal into the next best offer.

### Lead Systems Engineer

- Capture score, problem, path, audience type, and follow-up details.
- Do not lose leads.
- Preserve fallback delivery.

### Curriculum Designer

- Each course level produces practical artifacts, not passive content.
- Each artifact must show human review and problem-solving value.

### Badge Builder

- Use completion badge language only.
- Do not imply official school status, licensing, job outcomes, income outcomes, or complete compliance coverage.

## Current app pages and roles

- `index.html`: main conversion and free score.
- `answers.html`: answer hub for search and education.
- `checklist.html`: checklist request and lead capture.
- `booking.html`: human review and scheduling path.
- `lab.html`: recurring support and loyalty path.
- `content-engine.html`: daily value and audience-signal path.
- `thanks.html`: confirmation and next-step page.

## Business model alignment

The business model should not start with software-first selling.

Order:

1. Content teaches the market.
2. Free score diagnoses.
3. Checklist gives useful value.
4. Human review identifies fit.
5. Course or audit solves the first problem.
6. Badge proves reviewed completion.
7. Lab creates retention.
8. Sprint or implementation solves larger business problems.
9. Repeated signal decides what to build next.

## Required proof loop

Every paid path should collect:

- Problem solved.
- Artifact delivered.
- Human review step.
- Customer or learner feedback.
- Signal for next product or content.
- Next recommended path.

## Safe-claim boundary

AI Kollege should not claim:

- guaranteed income,
- guaranteed job,
- accredited certification,
- official license,
- automatic compliance,
- fully autonomous decisions,
- universal success.

AI Kollege may claim:

- practical training,
- proof-of-work artifacts,
- human-reviewed completion when reviewed,
- AI readiness scoring,
- workflow audits,
- safer AI habits,
- implementation support after scope review.
