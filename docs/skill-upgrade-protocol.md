# Skill Upgrade Protocol

## Purpose

Use this protocol whenever a skill feels weak, shallow, scattered, or disconnected from the real business mission.

A skill is not a slogan. A skill is an operating system. It must change decisions, pages, products, offers, scripts, follow-up, delivery, and proof.

## Upgrade rule

Every skill must include:

1. Purpose: what the skill exists to protect or create.
2. Trigger: when the skill must be used.
3. Inputs: what evidence must be checked first.
4. Operating principles: the standards it applies.
5. Failure modes: what weak behavior it must prevent.
6. Output: what the skill must produce.
7. Validation: how to know it worked.
8. App connection: what parts of AI Kollege must reflect it.

## The Boss Skill upgraded

The Boss Skill must not only organize work. It must decide priority by problem size.

Boss Skill asks:

- What problem are we solving?
- Who feels it urgently?
- What does it cost them if ignored?
- What is the smallest truthful useful solution?
- What proof shows it worked?
- What follow-up protects the result?
- What next larger problem appears after this one?

Boss Skill failure modes:

- chasing trends,
- overbuilding before payment,
- gold-chasing instead of problem-solving,
- weak positioning,
- unclear next action,
- no proof loop,
- no delivery system,
- too many offers without a problem map.

Boss Skill output:

- a prioritized problem,
- a clear offer,
- a clear customer path,
- a proof plan,
- a follow-up plan,
- a validation gate.

## The World Skill upgraded

The World Skill must not only be ethical. It must carry the full Jesus Pattern and Solomon Pattern.

Jesus Pattern:

- meet people where they are,
- tell the truth,
- love without using people,
- lead with authority,
- serve practically,
- sacrifice short-term greed for long-term trust,
- discern motive and risk,
- teach memorably,
- build community,
- judge by fruit,
- build beyond the moment.

Solomon Pattern:

- wisdom before wealth,
- order before scale,
- specialization before pride,
- counsel before isolation,
- reputation before arrival,
- alliances before trying to do everything alone,
- stewardship before appetite,
- succession/manuals before collapse,
- character before gold.

World Skill failure modes:

- spiritual language with weak conduct,
- ethics without effectiveness,
- wisdom without delivery,
- love without boundaries,
- authority without humility,
- revenue without fruit,
- content without transformation,
- AI tools without human dignity.

World Skill output:

- truthful positioning,
- customer dignity,
- clear service path,
- human review,
- proof-of-work,
- after-sale care,
- referral/community loop,
- durable trust.

## App audit rule

After skills are upgraded, the app must be audited against them.

Check:

- Does the homepage lead with the real problem, not just the product?
- Does each offer map to a painful problem?
- Are Jesus/Solomon principles visible through conduct, not religious decoration?
- Is AI Kollege the public brand everywhere?
- Is Fify Now LLC only the operator/legal layer?
- Are all public URLs aligned to aikollege.com?
- Are there any fake claims, fake credentials, fake urgency, or hidden internal notes?
- Does every page have a next useful step?
- Does every form support diagnosis, follow-up, and proof?
- Does validation guard against brand drift?

## Definition of best

The skill is best when it makes the app simpler, truer, more useful, easier to trust, easier to buy, easier to deliver, easier to refer, and harder to misuse.
