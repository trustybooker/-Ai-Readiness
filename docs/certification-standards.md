# AI Kollege Completion and Badge Standards

AI Kollege uses proof-of-work completion, not fake accreditation.

AI Kollege is the public brand. Fify Now LLC is the operator/legal owner unless changed later.

## Approved language

Use:

- AI Kollege Completion Badge.
- Completed AI Kollege Readiness Pass training.
- Submitted proof-of-work artifacts.
- Human-reviewed completion, if reviewed.
- Practical AI readiness portfolio.
- Operated by Fify Now LLC.

Do not use:

- Accredited certification.
- Official AI license.
- Guaranteed job credential.
- Guaranteed income credential.
- Compliance certification.
- College degree, university credit, or licensed professional status.

Unless those claims become legally and factually true.

## Professional completion requirements

A learner must submit:

1. Safe AI use checklist.
2. Problem-first prompt or workflow prompt.
3. Fact-check/review checklist.
4. Proof-of-work project.
5. Reflection explaining what AI helped with and what a human reviewed.
6. Role or business use case summary.
7. Human approval checkpoint map.
8. Problem-first summary: problem, user, cost of ignoring, smallest useful fix, proof collected.
9. Signal note: what question, objection, or next need appeared during the work.

## Badge levels

### Level 1 Badge: AI Kollege Readiness Foundations

Requires Level 1 deliverables and shows the learner understands safe basic use, review habits, privacy limits, and problem-first prompting.

### Level 2 Badge: AI Kollege Job and Productivity

Requires Level 1 plus job/productivity deliverables, including resume examples, interview answer, role prompt pack, one practical project, daily value note, and signal log.

### Level 3 Badge: AI Kollege Business AI Readiness

Requires business workflow, risk and trust snapshot, approval map, problem-size score, revenue/signal map, opportunity shortlist, and 30-day roadmap.

### Level 4 Badge: AI Kollege Implementation Readiness

Requires implementation scope, test notes, human checkpoints, proof and signal report, handoff guide, and support recommendation.

## Review rubric

Use a 1 to 4 score for each area:

1. Safety: avoids sensitive data exposure and unsafe claims.
2. Accuracy: includes fact-checking and correction habits.
3. Practicality: connects to a real role, business workflow, or daily task.
4. Human judgment: explains what AI drafted and what a person reviewed.
5. Clarity: artifact is understandable to a reviewer, employer, manager, or client.
6. Reuse value: artifact can be reused as a prompt, SOP, checklist, workflow, or portfolio sample.
7. Problem-first thinking: identifies the problem, buyer/user, cost, useful fix, and next step.
8. Proof and signal awareness: records what proved value and what question or need appeared next.
9. Truth boundary: avoids exaggerated claims about jobs, revenue, accreditation, compliance, or autonomy.

Completion passes when all required artifacts are present and no rubric category is below 2. Human-reviewed completion requires reviewer notes.

## Professional badge record fields

Each completion record should include:

- Learner name.
- Learner email.
- Badge level.
- Training path.
- Completion date.
- Version of curriculum.
- Artifacts submitted.
- Problem solved.
- Proof collected.
- Signal or next problem observed.
- Reviewer name or reviewer role when human-reviewed.
- Reviewer notes.
- Limitations or exclusions.
- Unique internal record ID.

## Anti-misrepresentation rule

The badge proves completion of an AI Kollege training path, operated by Fify Now LLC, and submission of practical artifacts. It does not prove accreditation, licensure, employment eligibility, compliance, guaranteed income, or guaranteed skill mastery.

## Public claim rule

Learners may say:

“I completed the AI Kollege Readiness Pass, operated by Fify Now LLC, and submitted practical AI proof-of-work artifacts for review.”

Learners should not say:

“I am officially certified by an accredited AI authority,” unless that becomes true later.

## Renewal and modern relevance

Because AI tools change quickly, public badge language should include the completion date or curriculum version. For ongoing professional relevance, offer annual refresh review or AI Kollege Lab membership instead of pretending one static badge stays current forever.

## Winning Skill alignment

Every badge level should point to a next useful action:

- Level 1: keep practicing safe AI use.
- Level 2: build job or productivity proof.
- Level 3: choose the first business workflow to improve.
- Level 4: maintain, review, and improve the implementation.

The badge is not the end. It is proof of progress and a pointer to the next real problem to solve.
