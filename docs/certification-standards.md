# Certification and Completion Standards

AI Readiness Pass uses proof-of-work completion, not fake accreditation.

## Approved language

Use:

- Fify Now LLC Completion Badge.
- Completed AI Readiness Pass training.
- Submitted proof-of-work artifacts.
- Human-reviewed completion, if reviewed.
- Practical AI readiness portfolio.

Do not use:

- Accredited certification.
- Official AI license.
- Guaranteed job credential.
- Legal compliance certification.

Unless those claims become legally and factually true.

## Professional completion requirements

A learner must submit:

1. Safe AI use checklist.
2. Prompt pack or workflow prompt.
3. Fact-check/review checklist.
4. Proof-of-work project.
5. Reflection explaining what AI helped with and what a human reviewed.
6. Role or business use case summary.
7. Human approval checkpoint map.

## Badge levels

### Level 1 Badge: AI Readiness Foundations

Requires Level 1 deliverables and shows the learner understands safe basic use, review habits, and privacy limits.

### Level 2 Badge: AI Job and Productivity

Requires Level 1 plus job/productivity deliverables, including resume examples, interview answer, role prompt pack, and one practical project.

### Level 3 Badge: Business AI Readiness

Requires business workflow, risk snapshot, approval map, opportunity shortlist, and 30-day roadmap.

### Level 4 Badge: AI Implementation Readiness

Requires implementation scope, test notes, human checkpoints, handoff guide, and support recommendation.

## Review rubric

Use a 1 to 4 score for each area:

1. **Safety:** avoids sensitive data exposure and unsafe claims.
2. **Accuracy:** includes fact-checking and correction habits.
3. **Practicality:** connects to a real role, business workflow, or daily task.
4. **Human judgment:** explains what AI drafted and what a person reviewed.
5. **Clarity:** artifact is understandable to a reviewer, employer, manager, or client.
6. **Reuse value:** artifact can be reused as a prompt, SOP, checklist, workflow, or portfolio sample.

Completion passes when all required artifacts are present and no rubric category is below 2. Human-reviewed completion should require reviewer notes.

## Professional certificate/badge record fields

Each completion record should include:

- Learner name.
- Learner email.
- Badge level.
- Training path.
- Completion date.
- Version of curriculum.
- Artifacts submitted.
- Reviewer name or reviewer role when human-reviewed.
- Reviewer notes.
- Limitations or exclusions.
- Unique internal record ID.

## Anti-misrepresentation rule

The badge proves completion of a Fify Now LLC training path and submission of practical artifacts. It does not prove accreditation, licensure, employment eligibility, legal compliance, or guaranteed skill mastery.

## Public claim rule

Learners may say:

“I completed the Fify Now LLC AI Readiness Pass and submitted practical AI proof-of-work artifacts.”

Learners should not say:

“I am officially certified by an accredited AI authority,” unless that becomes true later.

## Renewal and modern relevance

Because AI tools change quickly, public badge language should include the completion date or curriculum version. For ongoing professional relevance, offer annual refresh review or AI Readiness Lab membership instead of pretending one static badge stays current forever.
