# Certification and Completion Standards

AI Readiness Pass uses proof-of-work completion, not fake accreditation.

## Approved language

Use:

- Fify Now LLC Completion Badge.
- Completed AI Readiness Pass training.
- Submitted proof-of-work artifacts.
- Human-reviewed completion, if reviewed.

Do not use:

- Accredited certification.
- Official AI license.
- Guaranteed job credential.
- Legal compliance certification.

Unless those claims become legally and factually true.

## Completion requirements

A learner must submit:

1. Safe AI use checklist.
2. Prompt pack or workflow prompt.
3. Fact-check/review checklist.
4. Proof-of-work project.
5. Reflection explaining what AI helped with and what a human reviewed.

## Badge levels

### Level 1 Badge: AI Readiness Foundations

Requires Level 1 deliverables.

### Level 2 Badge: AI Job and Productivity

Requires Level 1 plus job/productivity deliverables.

### Level 3 Badge: Business AI Readiness

Requires business workflow, risk snapshot, approval map, and roadmap.

### Level 4 Badge: AI Implementation Readiness

Requires implementation scope, test notes, human checkpoints, and handoff guide.

## Human review standard

Reviewer checks:

- Did the learner complete the required artifacts?
- Did the learner avoid unsafe claims?
- Did the learner show human review judgment?
- Is the proof project practical and understandable?

## Completion record

Each completion should be logged with:

- Name.
- Email.
- Badge level.
- Date.
- Artifacts submitted.
- Reviewer notes.
- Any limitations.

## Public claim rule

Learners may say:

“I completed the Fify Now LLC AI Readiness Pass and submitted practical AI proof-of-work artifacts.”

Learners should not say:

“I am officially certified by an accredited AI authority,” unless that becomes true later.
