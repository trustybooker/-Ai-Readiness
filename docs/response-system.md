# Response System

Use these scripts to respond consistently. Replace bracketed placeholders manually. Do not promise jobs, revenue, accreditation, or legal compliance.

## Free score response

Recommended path: [Recommended Path]
Lead tier: [Lead Tier]

Reply with the main thing you want to improve first: job readiness, daily productivity, business workflow, team training, or implementation.

## Starter pass response

Your answers suggest that the best first move is the AI Starter Pass. This path builds safe AI habits before advanced tools.

It covers:

- What AI can and cannot safely do.
- Prompt basics.
- Fact-checking habits.
- Privacy and sensitive data rules.
- One practical work sample.

Next action: send the verified checkout link only after the payment link is created and tested.

## Job and productivity response

Your answers show a job and productivity opportunity. This path helps you create practical AI proof instead of vague AI claims.

It covers:

- AI-ready resume examples.
- Interview talking points.
- A practical proof-of-skill project.
- Prompt examples for your type of work.

No job guarantee is implied.

## Business audit response

Your answers suggest your business may benefit from an AI readiness audit before adding tools or automation.

The audit includes:

- Workflow discovery.
- AI opportunity map.
- Risk and approval checkpoint review.
- Training and implementation roadmap.

Before sending payment, ask for business type, team size, current tools, and the workflow they most want to improve.

## Team or implementation response

This looks like a higher-touch team or implementation opportunity. The right move is a short discovery review before payment so scope, risk, budget, and timeline are clear.

Ask for:

1. Business type.
2. Team size.
3. Main workflow problem.
4. Existing tools.
5. What success should look like in 30 to 60 days.

Then recommend the audit, team sprint, or implementation project.

## Follow-up cadence

- Day 1: respond with the correct path.
- Day 3: follow up with one clear action.
- Day 7: ask if they want the score interpreted or want the request closed.
- Day 14: close the lead if there is no reply.

## Closing script

I am closing the loop on your AI Readiness request. If this is still important, reply with “ready” and I will help you choose the safest next step.
