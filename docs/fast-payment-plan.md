# Fast Payment Plan

Goal: start accepting money as soon as possible without pretending the full Stripe integration is done.

## Recommended order

### 1. Fastest practical option: Square Payment Links

Use Square Payment Links first because it is simple, reusable, supports services/deposits, has built-in tracking, and can accept Apple Pay, Google Pay, Cash App Pay, cards, and other checkout methods depending on setup. This is the best first option for Fify Now LLC service offers because the same link can be reused and shared by email, text, social media, or embedded as a button.

Create links for:

- AI Starter Pass — $59
- AI Job & Productivity Pass — $197
- Business AI Readiness Audit — $497
- Team Training Sprint deposit — suggested $500 or $750 deposit
- AI Implementation Review deposit — suggested $1,000 deposit

After links are created, replace the website request buttons with direct payment links.

### 2. Good backup: PayPal Payment Links

Use PayPal if Square setup is delayed or if buyers prefer PayPal/Venmo/card options. PayPal payment links are also fast to share and do not require a custom website checkout.

### 3. Digital product delivery: Gumroad or Payhip

Use Gumroad or Payhip if the first product is a downloadable AI Starter Pass, workbook, mini-course, prompt pack, or certificate-of-completion package. This can be faster than building a full learning portal.

### 4. Full business stack: Stripe

Use Stripe after the offer is validated because Stripe is strongest for a professional long-term system: payment links, checkout, subscriptions, customer portal, invoices, coupons, and later custom app integration.
