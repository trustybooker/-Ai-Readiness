# AI Kollege Course and Completion Model

AI Kollege should sell practical proof, not fake credentials.

AI Kollege is the public brand. Fify Now LLC is the operator/legal owner unless changed later.

## Naming rule

Use:

- AI Kollege Readiness Pass
- AI Starter Pass
- AI Job & Productivity Pass
- Business AI Readiness Audit
- Team AI Training Sprint
- Local Service AI Rescue Sprint
- AI Kollege Lab
- AI Kollege Completion Badge
- Practical AI Readiness Portfolio
- Operated by Fify Now LLC

Avoid:

- Accredited certificate
- Official AI certification
- Guaranteed job certificate
- Guaranteed income credential
- Compliance certification
- Degree, college credit, or licensed professional status

Unless accreditation or legal approval is actually obtained.

## Core completion promise

The learner completes practical work and receives an AI Kollege completion badge showing they completed the training path and submitted proof-of-work artifacts.

The strongest professional positioning is:

**Score → problem → train → build proof → human review → badge → refresh.**

That makes the offer modern, useful, and truthful without pretending to be a university, licensing board, or legal authority.

## Winning Skill completion standard

Every level must answer:

1. What problem was solved?
2. Who needed the solution?
3. What was the cost of ignoring it?
4. What was the smallest useful fix?
5. What proof was created?
6. What human review was used?
7. What next problem or signal appeared?

## Proof-of-work artifacts

Every completion should include at least three of these:

- AI-safe-use checklist.
- Problem-first prompt pack for their role.
- Before/after productivity example.
- SOP or workflow map.
- AI-assisted resume or interview story.
- Business workflow readiness plan.
- Human approval checkpoint map.
- Daily value post or teaching note.
- Audience/customer signal log.
- Reflection: what AI should not decide without human review.
- One portfolio-ready artifact they can explain without exaggeration.

## Professional learning design

Each level should follow the same structure:

1. Outcome: what the learner or business can do after the level.
2. Problem: what real pain the level solves.
3. Concept: the principle they must understand.
4. Demonstration: one practical example.
5. Exercise: one guided task.
6. Artifact: one reusable proof-of-work item.
7. Review: what must be checked by the learner or human reviewer.
8. Signal: what question, objection, or next need appeared.
9. Next step: training, audit, sprint, Lab, or implementation path.

This keeps the course professional and scalable instead of becoming random lessons.

- Human review queue.
- Tool testing notes.

AI changes fast, so the membership gives customers a reason to stay connected and keep learning without AI Kollege becoming a custom dev shop for every client.

## User flow

1. Visitor lands on AI Kollege.
2. Visitor sees the problem map.
3. Visitor takes the free score or reads an answer.
4. Visitor requests checklist, review, or signal form.
5. Lead record captures path, problem, audience type, score, and message.
6. Human review recommends the smallest useful next step.
7. Learner or business completes the relevant level.
8. Workbook artifacts and proof are reviewed.
9. Completion badge is issued only with safe wording.
10. User is invited to the next useful path: Lab, audit, sprint, implementation, or refresh.

- personal readiness score,
- role-based proof-of-work portfolio,
- business workflow audit,
- human-in-the-loop implementation rules,
- refreshable completion badge,
- daily content engine,
- audience/customer signal loop,
- Lab membership for tool changes,
- implementation pathway for qualified companies.

This is modern because it connects AI literacy, employability, business workflow, governance, proof, content signal, and implementation instead of selling generic prompt lessons only.
