# Course and Certification Model

AI Readiness Pass should sell practical proof, not fake credentials.

## Naming rule

Use:

- AI Readiness Pass
- AI Starter Pass
- AI Job & Productivity Pass
- Business AI Readiness Audit
- Team AI Training Sprint
- AI Readiness Lab
- Fify Now LLC Completion Badge

Avoid:

- Accredited certificate
- Official AI certification
- Guaranteed job certificate
- Compliance certification

Unless accreditation or legal approval is actually obtained.

## Core certification promise

The learner completes practical work and receives a Fify Now LLC completion badge showing they completed the training path and submitted proof-of-work artifacts.

## Proof-of-work artifacts

Every completion should include at least three of these:

- AI-safe-use checklist.
- Prompt pack for their role.
- Before/after productivity example.
- SOP or workflow map.
- AI-assisted resume or interview story.
- Business workflow automation plan.
- Human approval checkpoint map.
- Reflection: what AI should not decide without human review.

## Level 1: AI Readiness Foundations

Best for beginners, employees, and job seekers.

Modules:

1. What AI can and cannot do.
2. Prompting for useful work.
3. How to fact-check AI output.
4. Privacy and sensitive data rules.
5. Human judgment and review.
6. One practical proof project.

Deliverable:

- AI-safe-use checklist.
- One practical AI work sample.

## Level 2: AI Job & Productivity Pass

Best for job seekers, workers, admin, support, marketing, trades, and operations roles.

Modules:

1. AI resume readiness.
2. AI interview stories.
3. Prompting for role-based work.
4. Email, document, reporting, and SOP workflows.
5. Portfolio/proof-of-skill project.

Deliverable:

- Resume bullet examples.
- Interview answer examples.
- Proof-of-work project.

## Level 3: Business AI Readiness

Best for owners, managers, and teams.

Modules:

1. Workflow discovery.
2. Automation opportunity scoring.
3. AI risk and privacy map.
4. Team training plan.
5. Human approval checkpoints.
6. Implementation roadmap.

Deliverable:

- Workflow map.
- Risk snapshot.
- Implementation roadmap.

## Level 4: Implementation Partner Track

Best for qualified companies that need guided execution.

Modules:

1. Business objective selection.
2. Tool stack choice.
3. Data and access boundaries.
4. Human-in-the-loop design.
5. Testing and rollout.
6. Metrics and review cadence.

Deliverable:

- Implementation plan.
- Pilot scope.
- Metrics dashboard plan.
- Support cadence.

## AI Readiness Lab membership

Purpose: recurring value and retention.

Includes:

- Monthly office hours.
- Weekly AI update brief.
- New prompt/workflow templates.
- Member examples.
- Human review queue.
- Tool testing notes.

Why it matters:

AI changes fast, so the membership gives customers a reason to stay connected and keep learning without Fify Now LLC becoming a custom dev shop for every client.
