What is AI readiness?

Direct answer: AI readiness is the ability to use AI tools safely, productively, and responsibly. It includes AI literacy, privacy awareness, workflow judgment, practical prompting skill, human review habits, and a clear implementation plan.

Check your readiness

How can job seekers prove AI skills?

Direct answer: Job seekers can prove AI skill by showing practical work samples: a prompt workflow, an improved resume example, a documented SOP, a before-and-after productivity example, and a clear explanation of how they checked AI output for accuracy.

Why train before automation?

Direct answer: Businesses should train employees before automating because untrained AI use can create privacy mistakes, low-quality work, bad decisions, and tool waste. Training helps teams understand safe use, review rules, and where AI should support humans instead of replacing judgment.
